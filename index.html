Why Blood Donation Matters

Donated blood helps patients who need emergency care, surgery, and other medical treatments. BloodLink brings essential blood donation information together in one place.

Before You Donate

Check Eligibility

Age, health, weight, and medical history can affect donation eligibility.

Prepare Yourself

Follow donation-centre instructions, stay hydrated, and get enough rest.

Consult Professionals

Qualified donation-centre staff will assess your eligibility and safety.

The Donation Process

  1. Register at an authorised donation centre.
  2. Complete the health screening.
  3. Donate under trained staff supervision.
  4. Rest and follow the aftercare instructions.
